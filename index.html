About Me

I’m Nox, a Computer Science master’s student at Georgia Tech, where I also earned my B.S. My focus is machine learning, and my current research explores RSI. I’ve also worked on LLM agents and reinforcement learning, including an AI internship at ZTE, where I built distributed training pipelines for multi-turn agents. I recently submitted a paper to ACL Rolling Review (ARR) and am in team matching for Google’s 2027 SWE internship. Outside of CS, I enjoy reading, Polaroid and film photography, drawing and painting, and visiting historic sites, especially ancient tombs.
