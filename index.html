About Me

I’m Nox, a Computer Science master’s student at Georgia Tech, where I also earned my B.S. I focus on machine learning and am currently researching RSI. I also have a first-author paper under review at NAACL. My work with LLM agents and reinforcement learning includes an AI internship at ZTE, where I built distributed training pipelines for multi-turn agents. I’m currently in team matching for Google’s 2027 SWE internship. Outside of CS, I enjoy reading, Polaroid and film photography, drawing and painting, and visiting historic sites, especially ancient tombs.
